The rule (labels.py, Label.join):
  trusted?  only if EVERY part is trusted     -> one untrusted part makes the whole value untrusted
  readers?  only who EVERY part allows        -> the smaller set wins ('anyone' adds no limit)
  sources?  all of them                       -> a refusal can name the email or file to look at
