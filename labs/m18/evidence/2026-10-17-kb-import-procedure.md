# Evidence: knowledge-base import procedure (sample for the M18 lab, fictional)

Since 17 Oct 2026, resolved tickets are no longer imported automatically.

1. The nightly job puts candidate articles in a review queue, never straight into the knowledge base.
2. A support lead rewrites each one in staff words; customer text is not copied.
3. Links are removed unless they point to the shop's own domain.
4. Every article records its source ticket ID and the reviewer.

Status: in place for new imports. The 412 articles imported before 17 Oct 2026 are being re-reviewed (148 done).
